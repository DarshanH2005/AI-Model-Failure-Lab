## Interpretation

Sensitivity measures response to controlled nuisance edits, not complete internal reasoning. Inspect all seed differences and ordinary-accuracy trade-offs before claiming a repair gain. Synthetic generalisation does not establish real industrial performance.

## Research interpretation

The primary comparison uses targeted and random nuisance selection with identical edit policies. Original checkpoint performance determines available headroom. If originals already attain perfect worst-group accuracy, no method can improve that metric; a tie is a ceiling-limited finding rather than proof that sensitivity-based repair never works. Regressions and matched-accuracy changes must still be reported.

All methods, seeds and budgets are preserved, including inconclusive or negative outcomes. Controlled sensitivity is not full internal reasoning. This synthetic proxy does not establish real industrial reliability. Perceptual near-duplicates remain possible; exact pretrained weight revisions/hashes are recorded for replay.
