Built Shortcut Detective with PyTorch, ResNet-18 and DeiT-Tiny; evaluated model robustness across 66 synthetic repair conditions and six external Waterbirds conditions with three seeds per architecture. Implemented controlled interventions, fair data-budget comparisons, calibration, group metrics and reproducible dashboards. Found ceiling-limited synthetic repair results and substantial external group disparities (mean worst-group accuracy 35.9% ResNet, 49.1% DeiT).
